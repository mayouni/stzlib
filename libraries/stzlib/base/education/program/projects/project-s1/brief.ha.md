# project-s1

> Fassarar farko ce, tana jiran bita daga mai magana da Hausa a matsayin harshen uwa.

Ƙaramin kayan aiki a kan wurin aikinka, sharuɗɗansa an bayyana su a matsayin bayanai.

## Abin da mai tsaro yake dubawa

- babban fayil ɗinka yana ɗauke da `rule.txt` (sharaɗi ɗaya, kamar `{ @item > 20 }`), `data.txt` (lamba ɗaya a kowane layi) da `tool.ring`
- mai tsaro yana gudanar da `tool.ring` da `$cProjectFolder` ya nuna babban fayil ɗinka
- kayan aikin yana buga `rule: ` sannan dokar da ya karanta daga `rule.txt`, da `count: ` sannan layukan bayanai nawa suka cika ta
