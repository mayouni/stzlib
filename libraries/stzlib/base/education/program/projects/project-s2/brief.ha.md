> Fassarar farko ce, tana jiran bita daga mai magana da Hausa a matsayin harshen uwa.

Rahoto a kan fayil na bayanai na gaske, mai siffofi, tebur da hoto.

## Abin da mai tsaro yake dubawa

- babban fayil ɗinka yana ɗauke da `data.csv` da `report.ring`
- mai tsaro yana gudanar da `report.ring` da `$cProjectFolder` ya nuna babban fayil ɗinka
- rahoton yana zana tebur (`stzTable.Show`), yana zana sanduna (`stzHBarPlot` ko `stzVBarPlot`), yana buga `pattern: ` sannan ko layin bayanai ya dace da siffar da ka rubuta
