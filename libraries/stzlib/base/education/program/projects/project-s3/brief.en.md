# project-s3

A world you taught, questioned by an agent you govern.

## What the guard checks

- your folder holds `world.zknw` (at least five facts), `agent.pia` (admitted by the court, every act reversible) and `ask.ring`
- the guard runs `ask.ring` with `$cProjectFolder` set to your folder
- `ask.ring` loads your world and prints at least one answer from it
