# FO-04 · Bayyana me, ba yadda ba

> Fassarar farko ce, tana jiran bita daga mai magana da Hausa a matsayin harshen uwa.

question: Zan iya faɗin abin da nake so in bar injin ya yanke yadda?
foundation: yana maye gurbin madaukin da aka rubuta da hannu da kira ɗaya da aka bayyana
practitioner: yana rubuta sharaɗi a matsayin bayanai maimakon reshe
expert: yana tsara ƙananan dokoki da mai aiwatarwa ke gudanarwa, ya bayyana abin da mai aiwatarwa ke yankewa
