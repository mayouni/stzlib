# FO-01 · Faɗi matsalar da manyan kalmomi

> Fassarar farko ce, tana jiran bita daga mai magana da Hausa a matsayin harshen uwa.

question: Me nake tambaya da gaske, da kalmomi kaɗan?
foundation: yana faɗin matsala a jimla ɗaya ya fitar da manyan kalmominta
practitioner: yana zaɓar manyan kalmomin da suke sunan abu na Softanza da tambaya
expert: yana sake tsara buƙata mara tsari ta zama tambaya da za a iya tabbatarwa kafin ya taɓa lambar
