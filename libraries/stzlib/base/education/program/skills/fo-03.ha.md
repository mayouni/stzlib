# FO-03 · Tsara abu ɗaya ga kowane tsari

> Fassarar farko ce, tana jiran bita daga mai magana da Hausa a matsayin harshen uwa.

question: Shin wannan tambayar da na yi wa jeri ce, yanzu na yi wa rubutu ko tebur?
foundation: yana yi wa rubutu tambayoyin Contains, Count da Find kamar yadda yake yi wa jeri
practitioner: yana zaɓar abin (rubutu, jeri, tebur, zane) da ya dace da bayanai, yana motsawa tsakaninsu
expert: yana rubuta mafita ɗaya da take aiki ga tsare-tsare da dama ya faɗi abin da bai canza ba
