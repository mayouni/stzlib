# KN-03 · Amsa tambayar giɓi

> Fassarar farko ce, tana jiran bita daga mai magana da Hausa a matsayin harshen uwa.

question: Me Softanza ta tambaye ni, kuma me ya sa?
foundation: yana amsa tambayar giɓin da manufa ta haifar
practitioner: yana bayyana manufa ya bar tattaunawar ta cika duniyar
expert: yana tsara manufar domin tambayoyin su zo a tsari daidai ga sabon shiga
