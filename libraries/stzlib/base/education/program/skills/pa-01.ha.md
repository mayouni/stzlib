# PA-01 · Gane siffofin rubutu

> Fassarar farko ce, tana jiran bita daga mai magana da Hausa a matsayin harshen uwa.

question: Menene siffar rubutun da nake nema?
foundation: yana karanta siffa ya faɗi abin da kowane sashe yake dacewa, yana gwada ta a kan kwanan wata na gaske
practitioner: yana rubuta siffa don takarda ta gaske (kwanan wata, lambar waya, farashi)
expert: yana gina regexuter da yake amsa abin da ya gane
