### Exercise 7.2 · One rule, two days


Monday's till: `[ 12, 0, 30, -5, 18, 25 ]`. Tuesday's: `[ 3, 40, 9 ]`.

Write the rule "above 20" **once**, as data, and print how many amounts satisfy it on each day, on two lines.

Your answer is checked by running your program. Nobody reads it and compares it with a model answer.
