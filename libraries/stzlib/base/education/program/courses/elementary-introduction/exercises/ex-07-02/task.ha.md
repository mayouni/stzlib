### Aiki na 7.2 · Doka ɗaya, kwanaki biyu

> Fassarar farko ce, tana jiran bita daga mai magana da Hausa a matsayin harshen uwa.


Akwatin kuɗin Litinin: `[ 12, 0, 30, -5, 18, 25 ]`. Na Talata: `[ 3, 40, 9 ]`.

Rubuta dokar "sama da 20" **sau ɗaya**, a matsayin bayanai, ka buga kuɗi nawa suka cika ta a kowace rana, a layi biyu.

Ana tabbatar da amsarka ta hanyar gudanar da shirinka. Babu wanda zai karanta ta don ya kwatanta ta da amsar misali.
