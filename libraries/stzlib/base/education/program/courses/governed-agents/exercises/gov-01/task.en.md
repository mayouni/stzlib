### Governed agents · Exercise 1 · A stock watcher the court admits

Declare, in a `.pia` file, an agent named `stock-watcher` that checks the kitchen stock every morning.

- It must say what it covers. An agent that states no reach is never scheduled.
- Every act it takes must be **reversible**.
- The court (`StzAgentDeclarationQ`) must admit it.

Your declaration is checked by running the library's own court on it. Nobody reads it for you.
