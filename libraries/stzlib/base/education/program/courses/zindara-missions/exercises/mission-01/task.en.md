### Mission 1 · Amina's customers

Amina is the baker of Zindara. This morning, these people came to buy bread:

    [ "Ibrahim", "Fatima", "Ibrahim", "Moussa", "Fatima" ]

Some came twice! How many **different** customers came to Amina's bakery? Make your program print the number.

You may write your program in English, French, Arabic or Hausa. It is checked by running it.
