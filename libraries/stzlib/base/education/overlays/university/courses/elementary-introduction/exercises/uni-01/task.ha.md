### Sahel University · Aiki na U1 · Abin da tsangayar take bayarwa

> Fassarar farko ce, tana jiran bita daga mai magana da Hausa a matsayin harshen uwa.


Koya wa Softanza gaskiya uku: `informatique` da `mathematiques` `faculty-of-science` ce take bayarwa, kuma `droit` `faculty-of-law` ce take bayarwa. Sannan ka tambaya waɗanne darussa tsangayar kimiyya take bayarwa, ka buga amsar.

Ana tabbatar da amsarka ta hanyar gudanar da shirinka. Babu wanda zai karanta ta don ya kwatanta ta da amsar misali.
