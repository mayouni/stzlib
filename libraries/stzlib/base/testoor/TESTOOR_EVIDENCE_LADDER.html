The evidence ladder

Testing shows that bugs are present, never that they are absent. So Testoor records how much is known about each node of the program's graph, property by property, on six grades. A change is accepted only when every node it reaches stands on the grade its floor asks for.

TESTOOR-LADDER-01: yes to all fourgrades 0 to 2 recorded todaynext step: V1

Six grades, read from the bottom up

Each grade is recorded per node and per property, never per file and never as a percentage. Higher grades cost more and say more.

A grade is only as good as its specification. A proof of a specification nobody read is weaker than a kept claim written from the real intent, so a floor that asks for proved also asks who read the specification (spec_read_by).

The steps, in order

  1. V1

    Report grade 2 for each node: kept claims joined to the nodes their tours reached.

    Next. Needs nothing from Haro.

  2. V2

    Property tours over declared domains, refinement bounds first, with a seed and shrinking.

    Needs nothing from Haro.

  3. V3

    Enumerate finite domains, and the schedules of the declared weather up to a depth.

    Needs nothing from Haro.

  4. V4

    min_grade and over: impact in the floor; hand-written goldens first.

    Needs nothing from Haro.

  5. V5

    The certificate record. Its first producer is Haro's checker: total, uses, the profile.

    Waits on HARO-PROOF-01 (a).

  6. V6

    Contracts, and a path from Haro to a solver or a verifier.

    Waits on HARO-PROOF-01 (b) to (d).
