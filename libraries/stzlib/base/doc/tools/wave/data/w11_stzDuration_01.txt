init | Builds a length of time from seconds, from text such as 1 hour 30 min, or from a hash list of parts. | nothing; the object is built | p=the length: a number of seconds, a text of numbers followed by day, hour, minute, second or ms units, or a hash list with the keys days, hours, minutes, seconds and milliseconds; anything else gives a zero duration | ParseDurationString, Components | the fraction of a second is lost today for a number or a text: 90.5 and 90 seconds 250 ms both answer Milliseconds 0; only a hash list with a milliseconds key keeps it, and Copy or Add then drop it | an empty text gives a zero duration

Components | Returns the length split into its parts, as a hash list of days, hours, minutes, seconds and milliseconds. | a hash list with the keys days, hours, minutes, seconds and milliseconds | | Days, ToString | a negative duration gives negative parts, which do not add back to the length | 93784 seconds gives 1, 2, 3, 4 and 0
ToString | Returns the length as clock text, hours in total then minutes and seconds in two digits, such as 26:03:04. | a text, h:mm:ss by default; ToStringXT takes a format of your own | | ToStringXT, ToSimple, ToCompact | | ToStringXT reads d, H, h, m, s and z for the parts and dd, HH, hh, mm, ss and zzz for the padded ones, where H is the hour of the day, h the total hours and zzz the milliseconds; a letter that touches another letter stays as written, and Duration and Content are the same call
ToHuman | Returns the length in words, such as 1 day, 2 hours, 3 minutes, and 4 seconds. | a text; 0 seconds for a zero length | | ToCompact, ToString | a negative duration answers 0 seconds | whole weeks and a few long spans have their own phrase: 7 days is 1 week, 14 days is 2 weeks, 30 days with 23:59:59 is 1 month, 183 days with 23:59:59 is 6 months and 365 days with 23:59:59 is 1 year, while exactly 30 or 365 days stay in days
ToCompact | Returns the length in short units, such as 1d 2h 3m 4s, leaving out the parts that are zero. | a text; 0s for a zero length | | ToHuman, ToSimple | | milliseconds are not shown; 100 days is 100d
ToSimple | Returns the length as h:m:s, or as m:s under one hour, with minutes and seconds padded by spaces. | a text such as 26: 3: 4 or  1:30 | | ToString, ToCompact | | the padding is a space, not a zero, so 90 seconds gives a leading space; ToString pads with zeros
