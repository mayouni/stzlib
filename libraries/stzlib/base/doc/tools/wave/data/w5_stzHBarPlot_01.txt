SetBarHeight | Sets how many rows each bar takes; the bar itself stays one row high and the extra rows are left blank under it. | nothing; the plot changes | nHeight=The rows given to each bar, raised to 1 when smaller | SetMaxHeight | The bar is not drawn thicker: SetBarHeight(2) spaces the bars out, and the Ring renderer ToStringInRing puts the blank rows after the last bar instead | the default is 1
