init | Builds an empty family tree that draws top-down with the family notation, with a title. | nothing; the object is built | pcTitle=the title of the diagram | AddPerson, Marry
AddPerson | Declares a person, drawn as a box with a name; AddPersonXT adds the years of birth and death. | the tree itself, so calls chain | pcId=the person's identifier, which must be new in the tree; pcName=the name shown in the box; pnBorn=the year of birth, 0 for unknown, taken by AddPersonXT; pnDied=the year of death, 0 for unknown or still alive, taken by AddPersonXT | Marry, AddNote | an identifier already in the tree raises an error; a year that is not a number, or a death before the birth when both are known, raises an error; the years are numbers, not dates | AddPersonQ and AddPersonXTQ are the same calls
AddNote | Adds a free note to the drawing; it is not a person of the tree. | the tree itself, so calls chain | pcId=the note's identifier; pcText=the text of the note | AddPerson | the rules and the kinship questions ignore a note
Marry | Joins two people in a union, a dot between the partners, and returns the union's identifier. | a text, the union's identifier, u1 for the first union, u2 for the next | pcA=the identifier of the first partner; pcB=the identifier of the second partner | UnionOf, Child, ChildOf, AddUnion | marrying a pair already joined, in either order, answers the union they have and adds none; an unknown person raises an error; the identifier is built from the number of unions, so it repeats an identifier you gave AddUnion by hand: after AddUnion("u2", ...) the next new pair also gets u2 and Unions lists two unions with that identifier
AddUnion | Adds a union under an identifier you choose, with any number of partners. | the tree itself, so calls chain | pcId=the union's identifier, which must be new in the tree; pacPartners=the identifiers of the partners, as a list | Marry, Child | one partner is a single parent and three or more is not a union: the rule union_has_two_partners reports both as a warning; an unknown partner raises an error
UnionOf | Returns the identifier of the union that joins two people, in either order. | a text; empty when the two are not joined | pcA=the identifier of one partner; pcB=the identifier of the other | Marry, PartnersOf | only unions of exactly two partners are searched
Child | Hangs a person from a union as a child of its partners. | the tree itself, so calls chain | pcUnion=the identifier of the union; pcChild=the identifier of the person born of it | ChildOf, Marry, ParentsOf | an unknown union or an unknown person raises an error; a person given two unions is reported by the rule child_of_one_union
ChildOf | Records a child of two parents, joining the parents in a union first if they are not joined yet. | the tree itself, so calls chain | pcA=the identifier of one parent; pcB=the identifier of the other parent; pcChild=the identifier of the child | Child, Marry | an unknown person raises an error | ChildOfQ is the same call
People | Returns the people of the tree, in the order they were added. | a list of hashlists with the keys id, name, born and died; 0 stands for an unknown year | | Unions, AddPerson
Unions | Returns the unions of the tree, in the order they were made. | a list of hashlists with the keys id and partners, the partners being a list of identifiers | | People, Marry
PartnersOf | Returns the people a person is joined to in unions, in the order of the unions. | a list of identifiers; empty for a person with no union or an unknown identifier | pcId=the identifier of the person, without regard to case | UnionOf, ChildrenOf
ChildrenOf | Returns the children born of the unions a person belongs to, in the order the unions and births were made. | a list of identifiers; empty when there is none | pcId=the identifier of the person, without regard to case | ParentsOf, DescendantsOf, SiblingsOf
ParentsOf | Returns the partners of the union a person was born of. | a list of identifiers, usually two; empty when no union was given for the person | pcId=the identifier of the person, without regard to case | ChildrenOf, AncestorsOf
SiblingsOf | Returns the other children of the unions a person was born of. | a list of identifiers, each once; empty when there is none | pcId=the identifier of the person, without regard to case | ParentsOf, ChildrenOf | only children of the same union count: half-siblings through another union are not listed
AncestorsOf | Returns every ancestor of a person, parents first and then up each line. | a list of identifiers, each once; empty when no parent is known | pcId=the identifier of the person, without regard to case | ParentsOf, DescendantsOf, GenerationOf | the order is the walk's, not the generation's: the last parent comes first
DescendantsOf | Returns every descendant of a person, children and then down each line. | a list of identifiers, each once; empty when there is no child | pcId=the identifier of the person, without regard to case | ChildrenOf, AncestorsOf | the order is the walk's, not the generation's
GenerationOf | Returns the generation of a person counted from the oldest known ancestor. | a number: 1 for a person with no known parent, then one more for each step down the longest line | pcId=the identifier of the person, without regard to case | AncestorsOf, ParentsOf | an identifier that is not in the tree answers 1
AsRuleGraph | Returns the tree as a plain graph that the five family rules read. | a stzGraph with the people, unions and notes as nodes and the partner and child lines as edges | | GovernanceFindings
GovernanceFindings | Returns what the five family rules find wrong with the tree, warnings included. | a list of findings, each a hashlist with the keys rule, where, severity and message; empty when the tree is sound | | GovernanceIsSound, AsRuleGraph | the rules are no_one_is_own_ancestor, union_has_two_partners, child_of_one_union, parents_are_older and partners_are_not_kin | CheckRules is the same call
GovernanceIsSound | TRUE if the rules found no error; warnings do not count against it. | TRUE or FALSE | | GovernanceFindings | RulesAreSound is the same call; a single parent is a warning, so such a tree is still sound
