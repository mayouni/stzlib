init | Builds a sorted list from a list, sorting its items once at creation. | nothing; the object is built | paList=the list to sort, of numbers, texts or both | Add | the list given is copied and the original keeps its order; numbers sort before texts, and texts sort by character code, so capitals come before lower case and Hebrew, Arabic and emoji come after Latin letters |
Add | Inserts one item at its place in the sorted order and keeps the list sorted. | nothing; AddQ is meant to chain and does not insert today | pItem=the number or text to insert | Append, Content | The Q form AddQ does not insert: AddQ("c") on b d leaves b d, because its body only returns the object; use Add | a duplicate is kept next to its equal
Append | Inserts one item at its place in the sorted order, the same as Add, since a sorted list has no end to append to. | nothing; AppendQ is meant to chain and does not insert today | pItem=the number or text to insert | Add | The Q form AppendQ does not insert: AppendQ("a") on b d leaves b d; use Append |
operator | Handles the + operator on the list: o + item inserts the item in sorted order, in place. | nothing; the list is changed | pcOp=the operator, only "+" is acted on, anything else is ignored; item=the number or text to insert | Add | the list changes in place, so o + 9 does not leave the original untouched
