Part B: Your project

B1 Project title

Fix it, don’t bin it: monthly repair sessions for Millbrook

B2 Describe your project

Since March 2025 our volunteers have repaired lamps, toasters, bicycles and clothing for local residents in the village hall. Sessions are free, and visitors learn to carry out simple repairs themselves. We now want to meet every month instead of every second month, and to train four volunteers to test electrical items safely.

B3 Who will benefit?

Last year 312 people brought items to our sessions, and two thirds of them were over sixty. We estimate that the extra sessions will keep about two tonnes of goods out of landfill each year.
