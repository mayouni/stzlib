No claim discount

Your policy includes a no claim discount for nine years. The discount is protected, so up to two claims in any three-year period will not reduce it. Protecting the discount does not protect the premium, which may still rise after a claim.

Endorsements

E07 — Approved repairer

If you choose not to use our approved repairer, an additional excess of £200 applies to any accidental damage claim.

E19 — Telematics device

Cover is conditional on the telematics device remaining installed and connected for the whole period of insurance.

General conditions

  • 1. You must take all reasonable steps to protect the vehicle from loss or damage.
    • (a) Keep the vehicle locked and the keys out of sight when it is unattended.
    • (b) Keep the vehicle in a roadworthy condition and hold a valid MOT certificate where one is required.
  • 2. You must tell us within 48 hours about any accident, loss or damage, whether or not you intend to claim.
  • 3. You must tell us before the change takes effect if:
    • (a) you change your address or the place where the vehicle is kept overnight;
    • (b) you want to add a driver or change the use of the vehicle;
    • (c) a driver receives a motoring conviction or a medical condition affecting driving.
  • 4. We may cancel the policy by giving you seven days' notice in writing to your last known address.

To make a claim, call our claims line on 01632 960 755, available 24 hours a day. Have your policy number and the registration of every vehicle involved ready when you call.
