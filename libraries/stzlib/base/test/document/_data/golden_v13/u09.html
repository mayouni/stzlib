Rahoton Shekara-shekara na 2025

Shirin kiwon lafiyar uwa da yaro a yankunan Dosso, Tahoua da Maradi

Taƙaitawa

A shekarar 2025, ƙungiyarmu ta taimaka wa iyalai fiye da dubu goma sha biyu a yankuna uku. Mata masu ciki dubu huɗu sun sami kulawa a cibiyoyin lafiya na ƙauyuka. An yi wa yara fiye da dubu shida allurar rigakafi.

Summary

In 2025 the organisation supported more than twelve thousand families in three regions. Four thousand pregnant women received care in village health posts. More than six thousand children were vaccinated.

1. Gabatarwa

Ƙungiyar Lafiya Tare tana aiki tun shekarar 2014 don inganta lafiyar uwaye da ƙananan yara a karkara. Muna aiki tare da ma'aikatar lafiya ta ƙasa, da shugabannin gargajiya, da ƙungiyoyin mata. Wannan rahoto ya bayyana ayyukan da aka gudanar daga watan Janairu zuwa watan Disamba na 2025.

2. Ayyukan da aka gudanar

  • Horar da ungozoma ɗari da arba'in a kan haihuwa lafiya.
  • Gina rijiyoyin burtsatse guda takwas kusa da cibiyoyin lafiya.
  • Rarraba gidajen sauro dubu tara ga iyalai masu ƙananan yara.
  • Shirya taron wayar da kai kan abinci mai gina jiki a ƙauyuka sittin.
  • Samar da babura huɗu don ɗaukar marasa lafiya zuwa asibiti.

3. Waɗanda suka amfana

Teburin da ke ƙasa ya nuna yawan waɗanda suka amfana a kowane yanki. Yankin Maradi ne ya fi yawan mutane saboda sabbin cibiyoyi biyu da aka buɗe a can.

5. Ƙalubale

Rashin tsaro a wasu ƙananan hukumomi ya hana ma'aikatanmu isa ƙauyuka goma sha ɗaya na tsawon watanni uku. Haka kuma, ambaliyar ruwa ta watan Agusta ta lalata hanyoyi da dama. Duk da haka, mun ci gaba da aiki ta hanyar ma'aikatan lafiya na al'umma da ke zaune a ƙauyukan.

6. Shawarwari don 2026

  • 1) Ƙara yawan ungozoma da aka horar a yankin Tahoua.
  • 2) Haɗa shirin abinci mai gina jiki da shirin allurar rigakafi.
  • 3) Ƙarfafa haɗin kai da rediyon al'umma don isar da saƙonni.
  • 4) Neman sabbin abokan hulɗa don ɗaukar nauyin motocin ɗaukar marasa lafiya.

Muna godiya ga dukkan abokan hulɗa, da ma'aikata, da al'ummomin da muke yi wa hidima. Ba tare da goyon bayansu ba, waɗannan nasarori ba za su samu ba.
