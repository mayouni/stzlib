1. Summary

SAFETY

September closed with one lost-time injury, in the processing plant. The underground section reached 214 days without a lost-time injury. Two high-potential incidents were investigated, and both investigations are complete.

PRODUCTION

Ore mined reached 186,400 tonnes, 3% above plan. Plant recovery fell to 88.1% after a flotation cell was taken out of service for five days. Copper in concentrate totalled 2,187 tonnes against a plan of 2,267 tonnes.

The lost-time injury on 12 September was caused by a manual lift of a load of about 45 kilograms. The investigation found that the overhead hoist had been out of service for two weeks without a replacement plan.

Corrective actions

  • Lifting in the processing plant
    • Repair the overhead hoist and add it to the critical equipment list.
    • Supply two mobile lifting frames for the pump workshop.
  • Ground control underground
    • Re-train scaling crews on level 9 before work resumes in the area.
    • Install additional mesh on the hanging wall of the main drive.
  • Repeat the chute unblocking procedure with every plant shift and record attendance.

All environmental results were within the limits of the water use licence and the air quality permit. The dust reading at the tailings facility rose after strong winds on 22 and 23 September. Water trucks have been added to the tailings access road during windy days.

6. Actions for October

  • Safety
    • Complete the lifting equipment audit in all workshops.
    • Run a fatigue management campaign for night shift operators.
  • Production
    • Recover 15,000 tonnes of the waste stripping deficit.
    • Replace the flotation cell agitators during the planned shutdown on 18 October.
  • Environment
    • Start the rehabilitation trial on the old waste rock dump.

7. Approval

This report was reviewed at the monthly SHE committee meeting on 5 October 2026. The committee accepted the corrective actions and asked for a progress update in November.
