Third Quarter Report 2025

For the quarter and nine months ended 30 September 2025

The cooperative recorded a surplus of 111 thousand euros in the third quarter, compared with 101 thousand euros in the same quarter of 2024. Grain sales rose by 15 percent because members delivered a larger harvest, while dairy sales fell slightly after the milk price was lowered in July.

For the first nine months the surplus was 131 thousand euros, which is lower than in 2024 because purchases from members grew faster than sales. The board expects the fourth quarter to be stronger, since most of the stored grain will be sold before the end of the year.

Members’ capital increased by 60 thousand euros after twelve new farms joined the cooperative in the spring.

Notes to the statement

Note 1 – Sales

Sales are recognised when the goods are delivered to the buyer. Grain sold under forward contracts is recognised at the contract price, even when the market price at delivery is different.

Note 2 – Other income

Other income in 2025 includes a grant of 22 thousand euros from the regional agriculture fund for the new drying equipment. The grant is shown as income in the quarter in which it was received.

Note 3 – Staff costs

The cooperative employed 31 people at 30 September 2025, against 30 a year earlier. Seasonal workers hired for the harvest are included in staff costs for the months they worked.

Note 4 – Depreciation

Buildings are depreciated over 30 years and machinery over 8 years, on a straight-line basis. The new grain dryer came into use in August and is depreciated from that month.
