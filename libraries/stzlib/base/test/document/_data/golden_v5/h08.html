PARENTS’ NEWSLETTER OF LINDEN STREET PRIMARY SCHOOLISSUE 14 · SPRING 2026

From the head teacher

Spring has arrived at Linden Street, and with it a busy term of trips, perform­ances and building work. I would like to thank every family for their patience while the new classrooms were being finished. The children have moved into the extension and the old portable cabins will be removed during the Easter holidays.

Our inspection report was published in February. The inspectors praised the reading programme and the kindness of our older pupils towards the youngest children. A copy of the full report is available from the school office.

Mrs Joanna Pereira, Head Teacher

A new library corner for Year 3

Thanks to a grant from the parents’ association, Year 3 now has its own library corner with beanbags, a carpet and more than four hundred new books. The pupils chose many of the titles themselves after a vote in each class.

Year 3 pupils testing the beanbags on opening day.

Mr Lindqvist, who teaches Year 3, said that borrowing has doubled since the corner opened. Pupils can take two books home each week, and the class keeps a wall chart of the books everyone has finished.

“Borrowing has doubled since the corner opened.”

Sports day moves to June

Sports day will take place on Friday 12 June instead of in May, because the playing field is being re­seeded. Races start at 9:30 and the picnic lunch begins at noon. Parents are welcome to bring folding chairs, but we ask that no dogs are brought onto the field.

Volunteers are needed to help with the egg-and-spoon race and to serve drinks. Please sign up at the office or on the sheet by the main gate.

Dates for your diary

  • 20 March – Spring concert, 14:00, school hall
  • 27 March – Last day of term, school closes at 13:30
  • 14 April – Pupils return after the Easter holidays
  • 4 May – Bank holiday, school closed
  • 12 June – Sports day

Healthier lunches from April

The school kitchen will introduce a new menu after the Easter holidays, with a vegetarian dish every day and fruit instead of pudding on three days a week. The menu was planned with a dietitian and tested by the school council in March.

Families who pay for school meals do not need to do anything. If your child has an allergy, please make sure the office has an up-to-date form from your doctor, even if you sent one last year.

Thank you, volunteers

This term more than thirty parents and grand­parents have helped with reading, gardening and school trips. Their support makes activities possible that the school could not otherwise afford. Anyone who would like to volunteer should speak to Mrs Achterberg in the office, who will arrange the neces­sary checks.
