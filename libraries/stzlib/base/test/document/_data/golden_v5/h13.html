Ratiba ya Chanjo za Watoto Child Immunisation Schedule

Chanjo humkinga mtoto dhidi ya magonjwa hatari. Mlete mtoto wako kliniki kwa wakati kulingana na ratiba hii. Kama umechelewa, bado njoo; mtoto atapewa chanjo alizokosa.

Vaccines protect your child against dangerous diseases. Bring your child to the clinic on time according to this schedule. If you are late, still come; the child will be given the vaccines that were missed.

Siku za kliniki / Clinic days

  • Jumatatu na Alhamisi: chanjo za watoto, saa 2 asubuhi hadi saa 6 mchana / Monday and Thursday: child vaccinations, 8 a.m. to 12 noon
  • Jumanne: kliniki ya wajawazito / Tuesday: antenatal clinic
  • Ijumaa: ufuatiliaji wa ukuaji na lishe / Friday: growth and nutrition monitoring

Baada ya chanjo / After vaccination

Mtoto anaweza kupata homa kidogo au uvimbe mahali alipochomwa sindano. Hali hii kwa kawaida huisha baada ya siku moja au mbili. Kama homa ni kali au mtoto hanyonyi, mrudishe kliniki mara moja.

Your child may have a mild fever or some swelling where the injection was given. This usually goes away after one or two days. If the fever is high or the child will not feed, bring the child back to the clinic at once.

Kumbuka: Lete kadi ya kliniki ya mtoto kila unapokuja.

Remember: Bring the child’s clinic card every time you come.

Chanjo zote ni bure. All vaccines are free of charge.
