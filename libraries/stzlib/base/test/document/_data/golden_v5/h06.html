1. Opening and approval of the agenda

The Chair opened the meeting at 18:35 and confirmed that a quorum was present. The agenda was approved without changes.

2. Minutes of the previous meeting

The minutes of the meeting held on 21 October 2025 were approved with one correction: the roof inspection is scheduled for 4 December, not 4 November. There were no matters arising that are not covered elsewhere on the agenda.

Daniel Kowalski asked whether the remaining maintenance budget would cover the roof repairs. The Treasurer replied that the repairs would be paid from the reserve fund if the inspection confirms the damage.

4. Courtyard renovation

The property manager presented three quotes for the renovation of the inner courtyard, including new paving, two benches and planting along the north wall.

  • a) GreenYard Landscaping: 27,400 euros, work to start in March 2026.
  • b) Hollins & Daughters: 24,850 euros, work to start in April 2026.
  • c) Stadtgarten Services: 31,200 euros, including a five-year maintenance contract.

Members discussed whether the maintenance contract offered in the third quote was worth the extra cost. Several members felt that the cooperative’s own volunteers could look after the planting.

Decision: The board agreed to accept the quote from Hollins & Daughters, subject to a site visit by two board members.

Vote: 6 in favour, 0 against, 1 abstention.

5. Any other business

Hannah Brooks asked whether the bicycle shed could be lit at night. The property manager will request a price from the electrician who serviced the stairwell lights in September.

Fatima El Idrissi reported that the residents’ newsletter will move to a quarterly schedule from January.
