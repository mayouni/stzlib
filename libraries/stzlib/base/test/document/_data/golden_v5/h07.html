AquaSense 300

Capacitive soil moisture and temperature sensor

Overview

The AquaSense 300 measures volumetric water content and soil temperature at the root zone of field crops, orchards and school gardens. Its sealed probe has no exposed metal electrodes, so readings stay stable for several seasons in acidic or saline soils.

The sensor is installed by pushing it into a pilot hole made with the supplied auger. A single cable carries both power and data, which keeps installation simple for irrigation contractors and extension officers.

Features

  • Measures moisture at three depths along one probe
  • Built-in temperature compensation from −10 to 55 °C
  • SDI-12 and Modbus RTU outputs on the same cable
  • IP68 housing rated for permanent burial
  • Factory calibration for mineral soils, with a user table for peat
Tip: Install the probe at least 30 cm from the nearest drip emitter. Readings taken closer than this mostly show the wetting pattern of the emitter rather than the moisture of the field.

Typical applications

Scheduling irrigation for vegetables and fruit trees, research trials on water use, and teaching soil science with live data. Several sensors can share one logger, and the logger can send readings to a phone every fifteen minutes.
