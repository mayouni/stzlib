RESIDENTIAL TENANCY AGREEMENT

Agreement reference RT/2026/0193

This agreement is made on 1 November 2026 between the parties named below. It creates a tenancy of a dwelling house and sets out the rights and duties of both parties.

TermTwelve months from 1 November 2026 to 31 October 2027
Rent£1,150.00 per calendar month, payable in advance on the 1st
Deposit£1,326.92 (five weeks’ rent)

2. Rent

2.1 The Tenant shall pay the rent by standing order to the account notified in writing by the Landlord. The first payment is due on the date of this agreement.

2.2 If the rent or any part of it is unpaid for fourteen days after the due date, the Tenant shall pay interest at three per cent above the Bank of England base rate. Interest runs from the due date until payment.

2.3 The rent may be reviewed once in each year of the tenancy. Any increase must be notified to the Tenant at least one month before it takes effect.

3. Deposit

3.1 The deposit will be protected in an authorised tenancy deposit scheme within thirty days of receipt.1

3.2 At the end of the tenancy the deposit will be returned within ten days of agreement on any deductions. Deductions may only be made for unpaid rent, damage beyond fair wear and tear, or missing items listed in the inventory.

1 The scheme’s name and the prescribed information are given in a separate notice handed to the Tenant at signing.

4. Obligations of the Tenant

4.1 The Tenant agrees:

  • (a) to keep the interior of the Property clean and in good condition;
  • (b) not to make any alterations or additions to the Property without the written consent of the Landlord;
  • (c) not to keep any animal at the Property, except one cat or one small caged animal;
  • (d) to report promptly to the Landlord any disrepair or defect in the Property;
  • (e) to allow the Landlord access at reasonable times on at least twenty-four hours’ written notice;
  • (f) not to sublet or part with possession of the whole or any part of the Property.

4.2 The Tenant is responsible for council tax, water charges, electricity, gas and telecommunications for the duration of the tenancy.

5. Obligations of the Landlord

5.1 The Landlord agrees:

  • (a) to allow the Tenant to occupy the Property without interruption, except as provided in this agreement;
  • (b) to keep in repair the structure and exterior of the Property, including drains, gutters and external pipes;
  • (c) to keep in repair and proper working order the installations for the supply of water, gas, electricity and heating;
  • (d) to arrange an annual gas safety check by a registered engineer and give the Tenant a copy of the record.

5.2 The Landlord shall insure the building against fire and other usual risks. The Tenant is advised to arrange separate insurance for personal belongings.

6. Ending the tenancy

6.1 Either party may end the tenancy at the end of the fixed term by giving the notice required by law.

6.2 The Tenant shall return all keys on the last day of the tenancy and leave the Property in the condition recorded at the start, fair wear and tear excepted. A final inspection will be carried out in the presence of the Tenant if the Tenant so wishes.

7. Notices

7.1 Notices under this agreement must be in writing and may be delivered by hand, sent by first class post or sent by email to the addresses given in clause 1. A notice sent by email is treated as received on the next working day.
