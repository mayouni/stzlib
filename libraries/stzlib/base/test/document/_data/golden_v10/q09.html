What changes on 2 November

  • The 06:10 journey now starts from Harbour Station instead of Quay Street.
  • School journeys serve Ashby School on weekdays in term time only.
  • The last evening journey leaves at 19:10, ten minutes later than before.

Buses on this route are wheelchair accessible and carry up to two folded bicycles. Please have your ticket or contactless card ready when you board.

During roadworks on Fenwick Road, buses may run up to five minutes late between the hospital and Ashby Village. Live departure times are shown at every stop with a display screen.
