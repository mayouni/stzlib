Prices were scored in proportion to the lowest compliant tender. Quality criteria were scored independently by two officers and the scores were then moderated.

Risks

  • Corran Clean’s price depends on a staff turnover rate that looks optimistic.
  • Silverbirch would need six weeks to mobilise, which would delay the start to May.
  • All three bidders assume the current opening hours of the building.

Recommendation

We recommend that the contract is awarded to Brightwell Services, which achieved the highest overall score. Its price is £13,450 a year higher than the lowest tender, but its method statement is considerably stronger.

Next steps

  • i. Committee decision at the meeting of 19 February.
  • ii. Standstill period of ten days, then contract signature.
  • iii. Contract start on 1 April 2026.

The current contract with the outgoing supplier ends on 31 March 2026 and cannot be extended.
