Allotments reopen after the winter

The gates of the Linden Row allotments opened again on the first Saturday of March, and by mid-morning more than forty plot holders had turned up with spades, seed trays and flasks of tea. The flooded lower path has been rebuilt with a gravel surface that drains far better than the old one. The work was paid for by the spring fair and carried out by volunteers over six weekends.

Seven plots are still free, and anyone who lives in the parish can apply for one. Half plots are available for newcomers who would like to try growing vegetables before taking on a full plot. Applications can be left in the box at the community centre.

Repair café moves to the library

After three years in the church hall, the repair café is moving to the reading room of the library, which offers more space, better light and a lift. The first session in the new room is on Saturday 11 April from ten until one. Bring broken toasters, lamps, bicycles, clothes and toys, and our volunteers will try to mend them with you. There is no charge, although donations towards spare parts are always welcome.

Bin collections over Easter

  • Good Friday collections move to Saturday 4 April.
  • Easter Monday collections move to Tuesday 7 April.
  • Garden waste collections restart on 13 April.

Meet the new warden

Priya Hollingworth started as our community warden in January. She grew up two streets away from Linden Row and spent eight years working for a housing charity in the city. Priya will be at the community centre every Tuesday morning for anyone who wants to raise a problem in person. She is especially keen to hear from older residents who feel cut off during the winter months.

“I want people to know there is somebody they can simply knock on the door of,” she says. Priya can also be reached by telephone on the number printed on the back of the residents’ directory.

From the residents’ committee

At its February meeting the committee agreed the following points:

  • – to ask the council for a pedestrian crossing outside the primary school;
  • – to buy two new benches for the village green;
  • – to hold the summer fair on Saturday 27 June;
  • – to look for a new treasurer from September.

The committee meets on the first Thursday of every month at half past seven, and all residents are welcome to attend. Minutes of past meetings are kept in the folder at the library counter.

Lost and found

A child’s blue scooter was left by the bus shelter on Mill Lane in February. A set of keys on a red ribbon was handed in after the litter pick. Both can be collected from the community centre office during opening hours.
