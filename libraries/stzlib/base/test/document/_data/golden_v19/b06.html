Interpretation and advice

Lead can accumulate in water that stands in lead pipes, and young children and pregnant women are most at risk. We recommend the following actions while the source of the lead is investigated.

  • Run the kitchen tap for at least two minutes before drawing water for drinking or cooking, especially after the building has been unused overnight.
  • Use only the kitchen tap for drinking water until the pipework has been surveyed.
  • Ask a plumber to identify and replace any lead pipes or fittings between the stopcock and the kitchen.
  • Request a follow-up sample after any remedial work.

A repeat sample taken after flushing would show whether the lead comes from water standing in the pipes.

Notes on methods
