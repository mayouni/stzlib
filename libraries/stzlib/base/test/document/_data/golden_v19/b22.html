CP-40 Series Close-Coupled Centrifugal Pumps

The CP-40 series is designed for clean water circulation in heating, cooling and pressure boosting systems. All wetted parts are made of stainless steel, which makes the pumps suitable for drinking water.

Applications

  • Heating and chilled water circulation in commercial buildings
  • Pressure boosting in residential blocks
  • Process water in light industry
  • Irrigation from tanks and wells

Controller settings

Pumps with the optional VC-2 controller are configured with a plain text file. The default settings are:

[modbus]
address      = 12
baudrate     = 19200
parity       = even
stop_bits    = 1

[control]
mode         = constant_pressure
setpoint_bar = 4.5
ramp_up_s    = 5

Changes to the controller settings take effect after the controller has been restarted.

Installation notes

  • 1) Install the pump with the motor shaft horizontal or vertical, never with the motor below the pump.
  • 2) Fit isolating valves on both sides of the pump to allow servicing without draining the system.
  • 3) Fill the pump and the suction pipe with water before the first start.

The pump must not run dry, not even for a short time.
