On the day

  • Bring your student card; you will not be admitted without it.
  • Only clear pencil cases and clear water bottles are allowed on desks.
  • Mobile phones must be switched off and left in the bag area.
  • Calculators must be on the approved list published by the School.
  • You may not leave during the first 30 minutes or the last 15 minutes of a paper.

If you are ill on the day of an examination, contact the School office before the paper starts.
