1 Summary

This report presents the results of the environmental monitoring programme carried out at the Kestrel Ridge Gold Project during the third quarter of 2026. Monitoring covered surface water, groundwater, airborne dust and environmental noise, as required by the conditions of licence EPL 4471.

The main findings of the quarter are listed below:

  • All pH and conductivity results were within licence limits at every sampling point.
  • Total iron at the downstream point SW-04 exceeded the licence limit in September.
  • Groundwater levels fell in four of six bores, consistent with the dry season.
  • Deposited dust exceeded the guideline at one boundary gauge for a single month.

2.1 Exceedance at SW-04

The September iron result at SW-04 was 1.12 mg/L against a limit of 1.0 mg/L. The exceedance followed a storm of 41 mm on 12 September, which carried sediment from the haul road crossing into the creek. The regulator was notified by telephone on 16 September and in writing on 18 September.

4 Dust and Noise

Deposited dust is measured with eight gauges at the lease boundary and noise is logged at the two nearest homesteads. The following points were noted during the quarter:

  • (a) Gauge DG-6 recorded 4.6 g/m² per month in August, above the guideline of 4.0 g/m².
  • (b) All other gauges stayed below 2.8 g/m² per month.
  • (c) Night-time noise at Hollis homestead averaged 33 dB(A) against a criterion of 35 dB(A).
  • (d) One noise complaint was received on 3 August and closed on 9 August.
