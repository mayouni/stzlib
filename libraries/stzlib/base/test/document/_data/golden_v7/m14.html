Part C – Declaration

I, the undersigned, being the Returning Officer at the election of a Ward Councillor for Riverside North Ward held on 15 October 2026, hereby give notice that the number of votes recorded for each candidate is as stated above.

I hereby declare that Tunde Paul Adeyemi (Progressive Alliance) is duly elected as Ward Councillor for Riverside North Ward.

The turnout across the ward was 47.8 per cent of registered electors. Rejected ballot papers were set aside for want of an official mark, for voting for more than one candidate, or for being unmarked or void for uncertainty.

Any election petition must be presented to the County Court within 21 days of this declaration.
