Annual Report of the Parochial Church Council for the Year Ended 31 December 2026

Presented to the Annual Parochial Church Meeting on 25 April 2027

Aims and Purposes

The Parochial Church Council works with the incumbent in promoting the whole mission of the Church in the parish, pastoral, evangelistic, social and ecumenical. It is also responsible for the maintenance of the church building and for the parish’s finances.

Worship and Ministry

Holy Trinity held 312 services during the year, including a weekly said service in the care home on Mill Lane. Average Sunday attendance rose from 86 to 94 adults, with between 15 and 25 children at the monthly family service. The Easter and Christmas services were again the best attended of the year.

Mission and Outreach

The council gave 10 per cent of its unrestricted income to mission, divided as follows:

  • Ashcombe Vale Food Bank, which the church also hosts on Tuesday mornings;
  • the diocesan partnership supporting two rural schools;
  • a winter night shelter run jointly with the other churches of the town.

Buildings and Churchyard

The repair of the north aisle roof was completed in September, two weeks ahead of schedule and within budget. The quinquennial inspection is due in 2027. The churchyard volunteers met monthly and planted a new hedge along the eastern boundary.

Reserves Policy

The council’s policy is to hold unrestricted reserves:

  • (i)equal to at least three months of unrestricted expenditure, currently about £25,000; and
  • (ii)a further £10,000 for urgent repairs not covered by insurance.

At the end of the year unrestricted reserves stood at £38,940, which meets this policy.
