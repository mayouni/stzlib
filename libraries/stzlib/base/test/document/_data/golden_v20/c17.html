1. Reason for Work

The crew of flight NW218 reported a BLEED 2 FAULT caution during the climb from Elmsford. The following ACARS message was received by maintenance control:

ACARS DOWNLINK   NWR218   23OCT26 0612Z
FLT NW218  EFD-LMR  FL190
MSG  ENG 2 BLEED FAULT
     BLEED VLV 2 POS DISAGREE
     CMD: OPEN    POS: CLOSED
     BITE CODE 36-11-42  CLASS 2
END MSG

Troubleshooting on arrival confirmed that the valve stayed closed when commanded open. The valve is to be replaced in accordance with the reference task.

2. Precautions

  • Make sure that engine 2 has been shut down for at least 30 minutes before you open the cowls.
  • Use heat-resistant gloves when handling bleed ducts and clamps.
  • Keep the work area clear of loose items while the cowls are open.
WARNING: Do not pressurise the bleed system while the duct clamps are loose. Escaping hot air can cause serious injury.

5. Findings

Both duct flanges were found free of cracks and heat damage, with light scoring within the limits of the maintenance manual. The removed valve has been tagged unserviceable and sent to stores with this card number.
