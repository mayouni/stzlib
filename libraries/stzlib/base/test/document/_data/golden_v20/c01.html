Schedule of Port Dues and Charges 2027

Effective from 1 January 2027 to 31 December 2027

This Schedule sets out the dues and charges payable to the Port Elmsby Harbour Board for vessels, goods and services within the harbour limits. It replaces the Schedule of 2026 in its entirety and applies to every call that begins on or after 1 January 2027. All amounts are stated in pounds sterling and exclude value added tax.

1. Application

  • 1.1Dues are payable by the owner, master or agent of a vessel, each of whom is jointly and severally liable for the full amount.
  • 1.2Gross tonnage (GT) is taken from the vessel’s International Tonnage Certificate; where no certificate is produced, the Board will assess the tonnage itself.
  • 1.3A call begins when the vessel passes the harbour limits inward and ends when it passes them outward.
  • 1.4A vessel remaining in port for more than 14 days pays a further 50 per cent of the applicable due for each additional period of 14 days or part of it.

2. Vessel Dues

Vessel dues are charged once per call on the vessel’s gross tonnage, at the rate for its trade and type shown in Table 1. Where the rate per GT produces a sum below the minimum, the minimum charge applies.

Goods dues are payable by the owner of the goods on everything loaded or discharged within the harbour, including goods moved between vessels.

4. Payment and Disputes

Invoices are issued on departure and are payable within 30 days of the invoice date. Interest accrues on overdue sums at 4 per cent a year above the base rate. A dispute about an invoice must be raised in writing within 14 days, stating the call and the amount disputed.

The Board may detain a vessel until all dues relating to it have been paid or secured. Nothing in this Schedule limits any other remedy available to the Board.
