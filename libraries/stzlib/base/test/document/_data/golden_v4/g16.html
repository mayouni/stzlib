Spring Bulletin of the Wendle Vale Cycling Club

Club News

The club welcomed fourteen new members over the winter. Most of them joined after the open day at the community centre in January.

The committee has ordered a new set of club jerseys in green and white. They should arrive before the first long ride of the season.

Members who have not yet paid the annual fee can do so at any Tuesday meeting.

Members' Corner

Ines Vardy finished her first two-hundred-kilometre audax in March. She says the hardest part was the last hour in the rain.

Our workshop evenings continue on the first Thursday of each month. Bring your own tools if you have them, and the club will provide the rest.

The next bulletin will appear in early summer.
