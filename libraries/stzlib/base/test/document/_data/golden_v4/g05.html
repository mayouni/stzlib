Field Trip Preparation

Pupils from Year 5 will visit the Dunmore Museum of Industry on 14 May. Teachers should work through the four lists below in the week before the trip.

Before departure

Complete these steps in the order shown.

→ Confirm the bus booking with the depot
→ Collect the signed permission forms
→ Print the route map for each group leader

What to bring

Each pupil should pack the following items.

• A packed lunch and a water bottle
• A waterproof jacket
• A notebook and two pencils

Checked on arrival

The group leader ticks each point at the museum gate.

✓ Head count matches the register
✓ First aid kit is present
✓ Emergency contact sheet is signed

Not allowed

The museum does not permit the following.

- Flash photography in the galleries
- Food outside the picnic area
- Large bags in the exhibition halls

Buses leave the school gate at half past eight and return by four.
