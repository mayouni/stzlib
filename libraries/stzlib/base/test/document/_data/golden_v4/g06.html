Registering a Community Garden Plot

The town of Elsby offers forty garden plots each season. Applications open on the first of February and close when every plot is taken.

To register, follow these steps.

1) Fill in the application form at the town hall.
2) Choose one of the three plot sizes:
i. small, four square metres;
ii. medium, eight square metres;
iii. large, twelve square metres.
3) Pay the seasonal fee at the cashier's desk.
4) Collect your key and a copy of the garden rules.

Plots are assigned in the order in which applications are received. A waiting list is kept for the following season.
