Maintenance Manual for the Orvale Grain Dryer

This manual covers the model D4 dryer installed in farm cooperatives since 2021. Read every section before opening the service panel.

Safety Precautions

The dryer runs on a three-phase supply and contains a gas burner.

Electrical Supply

Isolating the Main Switch

Turn the red switch on the left side of the cabinet to the off position. Lock it with your personal padlock before touching any wiring.

Checking the Earth Connection

Measure the resistance between the frame and the earth bar once a month.

Moving Parts

Keep hands clear of the auger while the drum is turning.

Routine Cleaning

Daily Tasks

Empty the dust tray at the end of each shift. Wipe the moisture sensor with a dry cloth.
