1 Summary

Good progress was made on the steel frame in September, and the first-floor slab was poured on 24 September. The project remains ten working days behind the contract programme because of the delay to the substation connection reported last month.

We have agreed a recovery plan with the electrical subcontractor that brings forward the first-fix work in the classrooms. If the network operator confirms the new connection date, we expect to recover five of the ten days by the end of the year.

There were no reportable accidents during the month. The school remained fully open, and all deliveries were made outside the morning and afternoon drop-off times.

2 Progress on site

2.1 Structure

The steel frame for the two-storey teaching block is complete, including the roof steelwork over the hall. Metal decking and the first-floor slab are complete in zones A and B; zone C will follow in the second week of October.

2.2 Envelope and services

Brickwork to the north elevation started on 15 September. Drainage connections to the existing school were completed during a weekend closure, as planned with the head teacher.

The clerk of works inspected the steel connections and the first-floor reinforcement before the pour, and raised no defects. Two brick panels were rebuilt after the sample panel was rejected for colour variation.
