1. Welcome and apologies

The Chair opened the meeting at 6.30 pm and welcomed Tomasz Wierzba to his first meeting as an elected member of the Board. Apologies were noted as above, and the meeting was quorate.

2. Declarations of interest

Ruth Calloway declared an interest in item 6 as a tenant of one of the blocks concerned and left the meeting for that item. No other interests were declared.

3. Minutes of the previous meeting

The minutes of the meeting held on 10 September 2026 were approved as a correct record, subject to correcting the spelling of a contractor’s name in item 5.

4. Finance report for the third quarter

The Treasurer presented the management accounts for July to September. Income was slightly below budget because three flats remained empty for longer than expected. The main concern was repairs spending, which was more than a quarter above budget after the emergency roof work at Mill Street.

(ii) to approve a revised repairs budget of £480,000 for the year;
(iii) to ask the Finance Committee to review the voids policy before the December meeting.

5. Repairs programme

5.1 Window replacement at Albion Court

The Property Manager reported that the tender for replacing the windows at Albion Court had attracted only one bid. Members were reluctant to award a contract of this size without a comparison.

After discussion it was agreed that:

(a) a second quotation would be sought from a contractor on the regional framework;
(b) tenants would be told about the delay in writing, and the letter would explain:
(i) the new expected start date of March 2027;
(ii) how to report draughts or broken catches in the meantime;
(c) the Chair and the Treasurer would be authorised to approve the contract between meetings, up to a value of £210,000.

5.2 Damp and mould cases

There were fourteen open cases, of which three had been open for more than eight weeks. The Board asked for every case to be inspected within ten working days of being reported, and for the age of open cases to be reported at each meeting.

6. Rent setting for 2027

Ruth Calloway left the meeting. The Chief Officer outlined three options for next year’s rent increase. The Board agreed to consult members on an increase of 4.5 per cent, which is below the regulatory limit, and to bring the final proposal to the general meeting in December. Ruth Calloway returned to the meeting.
