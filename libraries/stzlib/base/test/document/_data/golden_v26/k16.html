All specimens were received in good condition and were tested within the permitted tolerance on age. Strength is the maximum load divided by the nominal cross-sectional area of 22,500 mm².

The specified strength class is C35/45, so the 28-day results for both pours fall below the characteristic cube strength of 45 MPa.1 Specimen C-308 showed an unsatisfactory failure pattern and its result should be treated with caution.

The results relate only to the specimens tested.
