NV-CP 65-200 Centrifugal Process Pump

The NV-CP 65-200 is an end-suction, back pull-out pump for clean and slightly contaminated liquids. The rotating unit can be removed for service without disturbing the pipework or the motor.

Applications

  • Cooling and heating water circuits
  • Process water in food and beverage plants
  • Boiler feed at moderate temperatures
  • Irrigation and water transfer
  • Washing and cleaning systems
  • Condensate return

Design features

• Back pull-out design: the motor, bearing bracket and impeller come out as one unit.

• Replaceable wear rings on the casing and the impeller restore efficiency after years of service.

• Bearings are greased for life and sized for at least 25,000 operating hours.

• Casing drain and vent connections are tapped G 1/4 as standard.

Remote monitoring

Pumps with the optional sensor module report their operating data over Modbus RTU. A typical gateway configuration is shown below.

[modbus]
address  = 17
baudrate = 19200
parity   = even

; holding registers, read only
40001 = flow_m3h      ; scale 0.1
40002 = head_m        ; scale 0.1
40003 = bearing_temp_c
40005 = run_hours

Register 40004 is reserved and returns zero. Values are refreshed once per second.
