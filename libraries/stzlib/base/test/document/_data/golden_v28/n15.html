Annual Report and Financial Statements for the year ended 31 March 2026

Chair’s statement

This has been a year of steady progress for the Trust and for the 2,140 households who live in our homes. We completed 46 new homes for social rent in Oakworth and Lindley, the largest number in a single year since the Trust was founded. At the same time we kept average rent increases below the level allowed by the regulator.

Our tenants told us that repairs matter more to them than anything else. We therefore brought the responsive repairs service back in house in September, and the average time to complete a routine repair fell from 23 days to 11 days1. I would like to thank our staff, volunteers and board members for their commitment throughout the year.

Financial review

Total income rose by 10.4% to £8.66 million, mainly because of higher rental income and a capital grant for the Lindley scheme. Expenditure increased by 7.0% to £7.67 million. The resulting surplus of £986,000 has been added to reserves and will support the next phase of our development programme.

Notes to the financial statements

1. Accounting policies

The financial statements have been prepared under the historical cost convention, modified to include certain investments at fair value. The Trust is a public benefit entity and applies the Statement of Recommended Practice for registered social housing providers.

The principal accounting policies are set out below:

  1. Rental income is recognised in the period to which it relates, net of rent losses from empty homes.
  2. Housing properties are stated at cost less depreciation, and depreciation is charged on each major component separately:
    • structure over 100 years;
    • roofs over 60 years;
    • kitchens over 20 years and bathrooms over 30 years.
  3. Capital grants are recognised as income when the conditions attached to them have been met.

2. Staff costs

The average number of employees during the year was 87, an increase of 14 compared with the previous year. The increase reflects the transfer of repairs operatives into direct employment.
