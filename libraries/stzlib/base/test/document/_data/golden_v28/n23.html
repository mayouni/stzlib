SECTION 4: First aid measures

4.1 Description of first aid measures

  • After inhalation: move the person to fresh air and keep at rest.
  • After skin contact: remove contaminated clothing and wash the skin with plenty of water.
  • After eye contact: rinse immediately with plenty of water for at least 15 minutes.
    • Hold the eyelids apart while rinsing.
    • Get medical attention without delay.
  • After ingestion: rinse the mouth with water. Do not induce vomiting.

4.2 Most important symptoms and effects

Contact with the eyes causes pain, redness and a risk of permanent damage. Inhalation of spray mist may cause headache and nausea.

SECTION 5: Firefighting measures

The product is not flammable. Use extinguishing media appropriate to the surrounding fire. Firefighters should wear self-contained breathing apparatus.

SECTION 6: Accidental release measures

Absorb spills with sand or another inert material and place in a closed container for disposal. Prevent the product from entering drains and surface water.

SECTION 7: Handling and storage

Store in the original container at temperatures between 5 °C and 35 °C. Keep away from acids and from aluminium and zinc surfaces.
