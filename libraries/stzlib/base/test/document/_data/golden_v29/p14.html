Summary

Seagrass meadows store carbon, shelter juvenile fish and protect the shoreline from erosion. This project will measure how much light reaches the meadows of one estuary and relate it to the sediment carried by the river.

The estuary has never been surveyed systematically, although local fishers report a steady loss of meadow since the channels were deepened.

Objectives

The project has three objectives:

(a) to map the extent of seagrass meadows in the Skarvik estuary using drone imagery and ground-truth transects;

(b) to measure how sediment load affects shoot density, in particular:

(i) after spring floods,

(ii) near the two dredged shipping channels, and

(iii) in the sheltered northern bays used as a reference;

(c) to share a monitoring protocol that volunteer groups can repeat every summer.
