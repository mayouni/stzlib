3. Dividends

A final dividend of 6.4 pence per share for the year 2025, amounting to £3,072,000, was paid on 22 May 20262. The Board has declared an interim dividend of 3.1 pence per share, payable on 30 October 2026 to shareholders on the register on 2 October 2026.

4. Borrowings

The Group repaid £2.5 million of its term loan during the period in accordance with the agreed schedule. The revolving credit facility of £20 million remained undrawn at 30 June 2026, and all financial covenants were met.

5. Events after the reporting date

On 14 August 2026 the Group signed an agreement to acquire a specialist gluten-free bakery for a consideration of £4.2 million, subject to regulatory approval. No other events requiring disclosure have occurred since the reporting date.
