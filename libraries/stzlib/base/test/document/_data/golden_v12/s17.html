Endorsements

The following endorsements apply to your policy and change the cover described in the policy wording.

E04 Unoccupancy

If the home is left unoccupied for more than 60 consecutive days, cover for theft, escape of water and malicious damage is suspended until the home is lived in again. You must turn off the water supply at the mains whenever the home is unoccupied for more than seven days between November and March.

E11 Pedal cycles

Pedal cycles are covered under section C up to £1,200 for any one cycle, provided that the cycle is locked to an immovable object when left unattended away from the home.

E19 Home working equipment

Computer equipment used for business purposes is covered under section B up to a limit of £3,000 in total.

General exclusions

In addition to the exclusions in each section, the policy does not cover:

  • (a) loss or damage caused by:
    • (i) wear and tear, rot, fungus or vermin;
    • (ii) the gradual deterioration of the buildings or contents;
    • (iii) faulty workmanship or defective design;
  • (b) any loss that happens before the start of the period of insurance;
  • (c) loss or damage arising from:
    • (i) war, invasion or civil unrest;
    • (ii) radioactive contamination;
  • (d) any reduction in the market value of the property after it has been repaired.

Important information

You must tell us as soon as possible about any change in your circumstances, such as building work, a change of occupation or the home being let to tenants. If you do not tell us, your policy may not cover you fully and a claim could be refused.

You may cancel the policy within 14 days of receiving these documents and we will refund the premium in full, provided that no claim has been made.
