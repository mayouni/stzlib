Aerolux 300 Air Purifier

The Aerolux 300 cleans the air of rooms up to 45 square metres with a quiet, energy-efficient fan. It is designed for bedrooms, offices and classrooms where clean air matters every day.

True HEPA filtration

The H13 filter captures 99.97 percent of particles down to 0.3 micrometres.

Whisper-quiet operation

In night mode the purifier runs at only 24 decibels.

Smart air sensor

The sensor adjusts the fan speed automatically to the measured air quality.

Installation

  1. Place the purifier on a flat surface, at least 30 centimetres from walls and furniture.
  2. Remove all packaging and the plastic bag from the filter.
  3. Connect the power cable and press the power button.
  4. Pair the device with the mobile app by scanning the code on the back panel.
