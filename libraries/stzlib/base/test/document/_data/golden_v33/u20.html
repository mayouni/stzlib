Annual Report 2025

Management discussion

Revenue grew by 7.4 percent to 48.2 million euros, driven by higher electricity sales and the first full year of our grid-services contract. Operating profit rose to 14.0 million euros as the new wind park reached its planned output.

Investment remained high. We completed the second phase of the district heating network and began construction of a 12 megawatt solar installation on former quarry land. Both projects are financed from operating cash flow and long-term loans at fixed rates.

Net debt fell for the third consecutive year. The board therefore proposes a dividend of 1.80 euros per share, an increase of ten cents compared with last year.

Notes to the financial statements

The consolidated financial statements have been prepared in accordance with the accounting policies described below. All amounts are stated in thousands of euros unless otherwise indicated.

(i)Revenue is recognised when electricity or heat is delivered to the customer and is measured at the amount invoiced, net of value added tax.
(ii)Property, plant and equipment is carried at cost less accumulated depreciation; wind and solar installations are depreciated over 25 years.
(iii)Provisions are recognised for the dismantling of generation assets and are discounted at a rate of 2.5 percent.

The board of directors approved these financial statements on 12 March 2026 and authorised their issue.
