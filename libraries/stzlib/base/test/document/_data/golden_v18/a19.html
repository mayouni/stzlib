1. Summary

September production fell short of plan because of lower head grade from the western wall of Stage 4 and a two-day mill shutdown. Ore mined was 3% below plan, while waste movement was 5% above plan as the pre-strip of Stage 5 was accelerated. Year-to-date copper in concentrate remains ahead of budget.

Unit costs rose to 2.41 dollars per pound of copper because fixed costs were spread over fewer tonnes. The operation expects to recover the September shortfall in the fourth quarter, when higher-grade ore from Stage 5 reaches the mill.

2.1 High-potential incident

On 14 September a haul truck reversed into a windrow at the Stage 4 dump after its rear camera failed. Nobody was injured and the truck was not damaged. The investigation found that the daily pre-start check did not include a test of the camera display.

Corrective actions agreed by the investigation team:

  • (a) Add a camera and proximity alarm test to the haul truck pre-start checklist.
  • (b) Fit camera fault warnings to the dispatch system so that faults are visible to the controller.
  • (c) Brief all haul truck operators on dump approach procedures by 31 October.

3. Mining

Total material movement was 1,714 kt, of which 396 kt was ore delivered to the run-of-mine pad. Stage 4 supplied 81% of the ore and Stage 5 the remainder, from its first two benches. Drilling achieved 14,860 m against a plan of 15,200 m because one drill was down for an undercarriage repair.

Grade control drilling on the western wall of Stage 4 confirmed that the high-grade zone narrows below the 820 m bench. The short-term plan has been revised to blend this ore with Stage 5 material from October.

4. Processing

The mill treated 371 kt at an average rate of 571 t per operating hour. A planned two-day shutdown from 19 to 21 September replaced the ball mill liners and the main conveyor belt. Recovery was lower than plan in the first week after the restart while the flotation circuit was rebalanced.

4.1 Concentrate shipments

Three road shipments of concentrate left site for the port during the month. Moisture content stayed below the 9% transportable limit on every shipment.
