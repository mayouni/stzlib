Endorsements that apply to your policy

  • E04 – Unoccupancy: if the home is left unoccupied for more than 60 days in a row, cover for escape of water, theft and malicious damage is suspended until it is occupied again.
  • E11 – Locks: all external doors must be fitted with a five-lever mortice deadlock or a multi-point locking system.
  • E19 – Flat roof: the flat roof over the rear extension must be inspected by a qualified roofer at least once every five years.

Important information

Making a claim

If you need to make a claim, call our claims line as soon as possible and keep any damaged items until we have seen them. In an emergency, take reasonable steps to prevent further damage, such as turning off the water supply.

Cancelling your policy

You may cancel within 14 days of receiving this schedule and we will refund the premium in full, provided no claim has been made. After that period we will refund the premium for the unexpired period of insurance, less an administration charge of £25.
