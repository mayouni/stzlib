How to read your bill

The basic charge covers the cost of the meter, billing and the line that connects your premises, whatever the amount of energy you use. The energy charge is multiplied by the kilowatt-hours recorded during the billing period. Members on rate LP-2 or IR-1 also pay a demand charge based on the highest 15-minute load recorded in the month.

Notes

  • 1. On-peak hours for rate R-TOU are 2 p.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, excluding public holidays.
  • 2. The summer energy charge applies to meter readings taken from 1 June to 30 September.
  • 3. Members whose demand exceeds 50 kW in three consecutive months are moved to rate LP-2.
  • 4. A copy of the full tariff, including terms of service, is available at the member services office.
