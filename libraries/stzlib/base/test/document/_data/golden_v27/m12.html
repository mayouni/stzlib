PROGRAMME

Fourteenth Northern Coastal Hydrology Symposium

9–10 June 2026 · Marrowick Conference Centre

The symposium brings together hydrologists, engineers and planners who work on estuaries, tidal rivers and low-lying coastal land. This year the programme gives particular attention to compound flooding, where storm surge and river discharge arrive together.

Parallel sessions run in three rooms on both days. Speakers are asked to load their slides in the preparation room at least one hour before their session.
