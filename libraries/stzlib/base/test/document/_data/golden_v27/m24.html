Roof repairs finally under way

by the Maintenance Group

After two wet winters and a long search for a contractor, work on the roofs of numbers 2 to 18 started on 5 October. Scaffolding will move along the terrace in three stages, and each stage should take about four weeks if the weather is kind.

Residents whose homes are covered by scaffolding will receive a letter a week before it goes up. Please keep windows on the scaffolded side closed during working hours and park away from the skip on Pottery Lane.

A new committee for 2027

Four of our seven committee members will stand down at the general meeting in December, after many years of service. Any member who has lived in the co-operative for at least a year can be nominated. Nomination forms are in the community room and must reach the secretary by 19 November.

Garden corner

The tulips and daffodils that brightened the communal garden last spring came from a single morning of planting. This year we have three hundred bulbs to plant, so bring gloves, a trowel and a friend on 7 November. Tea and cake will be provided by the garden group.

From the members’ meeting

Thirty-eight households were represented at the meeting on 24 September. Members decided:

1)
to replace the entry-phone system at numbers 20 to 36 next spring;
2)
to give the youth club the use of the community room on Friday evenings;
3)
to set aside £1,500 for new bicycle stands near the garden gate.
