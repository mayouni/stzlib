Section D – Conditions of hire

  1. The hirer must be aged 18 or over and must be present throughout the booking.
  2. The hirer is responsible for:
    1. the behaviour of all guests;
    2. leaving the hall clean and tidy, with all rubbish removed;
    3. switching off lights and heating and locking all doors.
  3. No more than the stated number of people may be in a room at any time.
  4. Decorations must not be fixed to walls or ceilings with nails, tape or adhesive putty.
  5. Bookings cancelled less than 7 days before the event will be charged in full.
