Guidance for New Committee Members

Joining a committee brings new re­spon­si­bil­i­ties for every member. The ad­min­is­tra­tion of the association depends on clear com­mu­ni­ca­tion between its officers. Members should read the minutes of previous meetings before their first session.

Good un­der­stand­ing of the budget is expected from the treasurer and the chair. Every decision about the shared in­fra­struc­ture requires careful con­sid­er­a­tion of its en­vi­ron­men­tal impact. Active par­tic­i­pa­tion in working groups is encouraged but not required.

The secretary is responsible for the im­ple­men­ta­tion of agreed actions. Questions about the or­gan­i­sa­tion of meetings may be sent to the secretary at any time.
