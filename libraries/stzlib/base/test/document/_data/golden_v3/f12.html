Preparing the Community Hall for the Winter Fair

The fair opens on the first Saturday of December. Volunteers should arrive one hour before the doors open. The caretaker will unlock the side entrance at eight o'clock.

What to Bring

  • Extension cables and a power strip
  • Table covers in plain colours
  • A cash box with small change
    • Coins for the raffle stall
    • A receipt book
  • Printed price labels

Before You Leave

  • → Switch off the heating in the main hall
  • → Stack the chairs along the north wall
  • → Return the keys to the office of the caretaker
  • → Sign the volunteer sheet at the entrance

Thank you for helping to make the fair a success. Please report any damage to the coordinator before you go home.
