Preparation of Luria–Bertani (LB) Agar Plates

1 Purpose

This procedure describes the preparation, sterilisation and pouring of LB agar plates for routine bacterial culture.

2 Scope

It applies to all staff and students who prepare media in the Media Preparation Unit.

3 Safety

  • Wear a laboratory coat, safety glasses and heat-resistant gloves when handling hot media.
  • Never open the autoclave until the chamber pressure has returned to zero.
  • Report any burn or spillage to the unit supervisor immediately.

4 Materials

Component Amount per litre Grade
Tryptone 10 g Microbiology grade
Yeast extract 5 g Microbiology grade
Sodium chloride 10 g Analytical reagent
Agar 15 g Bacteriological
Purified water to 1000 mL Type II

5 Procedure

  1. Weigh the dry components into a 2 L glass bottle.
  2. Add 900 mL of purified water and stir until the powders are fully suspended.
  3. Adjust the volume to 1000 mL.
  4. Sterilise by autoclaving:
    1. 121 °C for 15 minutes on the liquid cycle;
    2. loosen the cap by a quarter turn before loading.
  5. Cool the medium to about 50 °C in a water bath.
  6. If required, add the antibiotic aseptically and swirl gently to mix.
  7. Pour approximately 20 mL into each sterile Petri dish and allow the plates to set.

6 Storage and records

Store the plates inverted at 2–8 °C and use them within four weeks. Record the batch number, date and preparer in the media log. Discard any plate that shows contamination, cracking or drying.
