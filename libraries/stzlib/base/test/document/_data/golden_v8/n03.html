Diagnosis

Primary: community-acquired pneumonia, right lower lobe.
Secondary: acute kidney injury, stage 1, resolved.

Reason for admission

Mrs Marsh-Okafor presented with four days of productive cough, fever and increasing breathlessness. On arrival she was hypoxic, with oxygen saturations of 88% on room air, and a chest radiograph showed right lower lobe consolidation.

Clinical course

She was treated with intravenous co-amoxiclav and oral clarithromycin, and the antibiotics were switched to tablets on day four. Her kidney function worsened on admission and recovered with intravenous fluids and the temporary withholding of ramipril. Supplementary oxygen was weaned off by day five, and she walked independently on the ward before discharge.
