About us

LARL is a non-profit organisation that helps smallholder farmers around Lake Kyoga to raise their incomes and adapt to a changing climate. We currently work with 18,000 households in five districts.

Key responsibilities

  • Maintain the programme’s results framework and indicator reference sheets.
  • Plan and supervise baseline, midline and endline surveys.
  • Train field officers in data collection and data quality checks.
  • Prepare quarterly progress reports and dashboards for donors and partners.
  • Organise learning reviews with farmer groups and district officials.

Qualifications and experience

  • A bachelor’s degree in statistics, economics, agriculture or a related field
  • At least three years of experience in monitoring and evaluation, including:
    • designing household surveys and sampling plans
    • managing data collection on mobile phones or tablets
  • Strong skills in spreadsheets and in at least one statistical package
  • Fluency in English; knowledge of Ateso or Lusoga is an advantage

How to apply

Send a cover letter and a CV of no more than three pages to jobs@larl.example, quoting the reference number in the subject line. Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted.
