Lifeboat Day Raises Record Sum

More than 900 people lined the quay on a bright September Saturday for the annual Lifeboat Day. Together they raised £6,420, the largest sum in the event’s forty-year history.

The crew demonstrated a launch at noon, and the children’s raft race that followed drew the loudest cheers of the day. Volunteers served more than three hundred portions of fish and chips.

Thanks to our helpers

The organisers thank the harbour office, the sailing club and the many local shops that donated prizes for the raffle.

New Ferry Timetable from October

From 5 October the passenger ferry will run a winter timetable with fewer sailings on weekends but an extra early crossing on weekdays.

  • Weekdays: first sailing at 06.45, last at 21.15
  • Saturdays: first sailing at 08.30, last at 19.30
  • Sundays: first sailing at 10.00, last at 17.00

Season tickets remain valid and can be renewed at the harbour office.

Letters to the Editor

Dear Editor, I would like to thank whoever repaired the footbridge over the creek. It has made the walk to school much safer for the children.

A further note: the lamp at the corner of Quay Street has been out for three weeks and the Parish Council has been informed.

Gardening Corner

October is the time to plant spring bulbs. A few simple rules will give the best results:

1. Plant bulbs at a depth of three times their height.

2. Choose a spot with good drainage and some sun.

3. Water once after planting and then leave them alone.

Harbour Notes

The harbour office reminds all boat owners that winter mooring fees are due by the end of October. Owners who have not yet received an invoice should call the office during opening hours.

Work on the new slipway will begin in November and is expected to last six weeks. During this time the north quay will be closed to vehicles, and a temporary footpath will be marked along the sea wall.

Dredging update

The council has confirmed that dredging of the inner basin will take place in the spring, once the bird-nesting season has ended.

Recipe of the Season

Mrs Odell’s fish pie has won the village bake-off three years running. She has kindly agreed to share her method.

  1. Poach 600 g of mixed white fish and smoked haddock in milk for ten minutes.
  2. Make a white sauce with the strained milk, then fold in the flaked fish and a handful of parsley.
  3. Cover with mashed potato and bake at 200 degrees for thirty minutes.

Serve with peas and a wedge of lemon.
