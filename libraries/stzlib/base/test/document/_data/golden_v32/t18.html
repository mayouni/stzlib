Item 3: planning applications

The committee considered the following applications and agreed the comments shown.

(a) 26/0412, extension to the rear of 14 Mill Road: no objection.

(b) 26/0455, change of use of the former dairy, Church Street: objection on parking grounds.

(c) 26/0471, felling of two ash trees at Glebe Field: no objection, subject to replanting.

Decisions

  1. The committee resolved to submit the comments above to the district council by 19 June.
  2. The committee supported the footpath diversion in principle, on two conditions:
    • the new route must be surfaced and waymarked;
    • the old route must remain open until the work is complete.
  3. The next meeting will be held on Tuesday 14 July 2026.

The chair thanked the members of the public for their contributions and closed the meeting at 8.25 pm.
