Section 4: Leave and Absence

This section explains the types of leave available to employees of Crestwood Logistics Ltd and how to request them. All leave is subject to the approval of the line manager unless stated otherwise.

4.1 Annual leave

Leave years run from 1 January to 31 December. Entitlement depends on length of service, as set out below.

Length of serviceDays per yearCarry-over limit (days)
Under 2 years225
2 to 5 years255
5 to 10 years277
Over 10 years3010

How to request leave

  1. Submit the request in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance.
  2. Wait for written approval from your line manager before booking travel.
  3. If your request is declined, you may ask for a review:
    1. The review is carried out by the next level of management.
    2. You will receive a written answer within five working days.
  4. Record any approved change in the shared team calendar.

Employees who leave the company are paid for any untaken leave on their final payslip.

4.2 Sick leave

Employees who are unable to work because of illness must tell their line manager as early as possible on the first day of absence.

(a) Absences of up to seven calendar days require a self-certification form.
(b) Absences of more than seven calendar days require a fit note from a doctor. The fit note must:
(i) state the expected duration of the absence;
(ii) be sent to the HR department within three days of issue.
(c) Repeated short absences may lead to a supportive review meeting.

Terms used

Qualifying dayA day on which the employee would normally have worked.
Fit noteA statement from a doctor on whether the employee is fit for work.
Self-certificationA form in which the employee confirms the reason for a short absence.

4.4 Compassionate leave

Up to five days of paid compassionate leave are granted on the death of a close relative. Further unpaid leave may be agreed with the line manager.

4.5 Public holidays

The company observes the following arrangements for public holidays:

  1. Offices are closed on all public holidays.
  2. Warehouse staff who work on a public holiday receive a day off in lieu within 30 days.
  3. Public holidays are not deducted from annual leave.

4.6 Contact

Questions about this section should be sent to the HR service desk, which is open on working days from 8.30 am to 5.00 pm.
