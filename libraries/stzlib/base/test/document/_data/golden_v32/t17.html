Hartwell Marine Holdings plc

Consolidated Financial Statements for the Year Ended 31 December 2025

The directors present the consolidated financial statements of Hartwell Marine Holdings plc and its subsidiaries for the year ended 31 December 2025. Group revenue grew by 6.9% to £482.6 million, and profit for the year rose by 16.7% to £56.6 million.

Basis of preparation

The statements have been prepared in accordance with the following principles:

  1. The financial statements are prepared under the historical cost convention.
  2. Amounts are presented in pounds sterling and rounded to the nearest £0.1 million.
  3. Comparative figures for 2024 have been restated only where stated in the notes.

Key accounting policies

ConsolidationThe statements include all entities controlled by the company at the year end.
RevenueRevenue is recognised when the voyage or service has been delivered to the customer.
Property, plant and equipmentVessels are depreciated on a straight-line basis over 25 years.

The group applies the following exceptions to these policies:

(a) Dry-docking costs are capitalised and depreciated over the period to the next docking.

(b) Short-term leases of less than twelve months are expensed as incurred.

These statements were approved by the board of directors on 12 March 2026.
