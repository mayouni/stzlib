INDUSTRIAL COMPRESSED AIR

LX-240 Industrial Air Compressor

Key features

  • Oil-lubricated twin-cylinder pump
  • Thermal overload protection
  • Low-noise operation at 68 dB(A)
  • 50 l galvanised tank

Certifications

• CE marking

• EN 1012-1

• RoHS compliant

The LX-240 is a compact, belt-driven compressor for workshops and small production lines. It delivers 240 litres of free air per minute at 8 bar and starts reliably at low temperatures.
