Ground Handling Bulletin GHB-26-14: Winter De-icing and Anti-icing Operations

Effective date1 November 2026
Applies toAll stations; ATR 72-600 and Dash 8-400 fleets
SupersedesGHB-25-09
Issued byHead of Ground Operations

1. Purpose

This bulletin sets out the procedures that all stations and contracted handling agents must follow when aircraft are de-iced or anti-iced during the 2026–27 winter season. It replaces the previous bulletin in full and must be read together with the Ground Operations Manual, chapter 9.

2. Responsibilities

•The station manager ensures that only trained and authorised staff apply fluids.
•The de-icing lead decides the treatment method and records the start time of the final step.
•The commander remains responsible for deciding whether the aircraft is fit to take off.

3. Holdover times

Holdover time is the estimated period during which the anti-icing fluid prevents frost, ice or snow from forming on the treated surfaces. The table below is reproduced for training purposes; in operation, always use the current holdover-time guidelines issued for the season.

4. Communication with the flight crew

Before the aircraft leaves the de-icing pad, the de-icing lead must pass the anti-icing code to the commander by radio or by ACARS. Messages sent by ACARS must follow the format below exactly.

DEICE/ANTI-ICE REPORT
FLT KRA2417   ACFT G-ZKRA   STN EXT
FLUID   TYPE IV 100/0
START   0642Z (FINAL STEP)
INSP    POST-TREATMENT CHECK COMPLETED
CODE    IV/100/0642/COMPLETE

The commander confirms receipt by repeating the fluid type and the start time.

5. Pre-departure checklist

  • Contamination check carried out on wings, tail and control surfaces
  • Fluid type and concentration confirmed against the batch sheet
  • Start time of the final step recorded
  • Anti-icing code passed to the commander
  • Post-treatment check signed by the de-icing lead

6. Reporting

Any fluid spill, equipment failure or missed holdover time must be reported through the safety reporting system before the end of the shift. Stations send a weekly summary of de-icing movements to Ground Operations every Monday.

Approved by: Head of Ground Operations, 20 October 2026
