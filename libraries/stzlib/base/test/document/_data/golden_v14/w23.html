Lowmoor Folk & Lantern Festival · 16–18 July 2027

Welcome

Thank you for volunteering at the twelfth Lowmoor Folk & Lantern Festival. More than four hundred volunteers make the festival possible, from the people who build the lantern trail to those who greet visitors at the gate. This handbook explains what we expect of you and what you can expect from us.

Please read it before your first shift and keep it with you during the festival weekend.

1. Before the festival

1.1 Registration

Every volunteer must complete the online registration form and choose at least three shifts. Volunteers under 18 need a signed consent form from a parent or guardian.

4. Health and safety

Your safety and the safety of visitors come first. Wear your volunteer tabard at all times while on duty, and drink plenty of water in hot weather.

  • Report any hazard to your team leader straight away.
  • Do not lift anything heavier than you can manage comfortably.
  • Never block an emergency exit or a fire lane.

In an emergency

Call the control room on channel 1 of your radio, or dial the number on the back of your pass. Stay with the casualty until a first-aider arrives.

Lost children

Take the child to the welfare tent next to the main gate and stay with them. Never leave a lost child alone, even for a moment.

5. Code of conduct

As a volunteer you represent the festival, and we ask you to follow these rules:

(i)Treat visitors, artists and other volunteers with courtesy and respect.
(ii)Do not drink alcohol before or during a shift.
(iii)Do not post photographs of children or of the backstage area on social media.
(iv)Tell your team leader if you cannot attend a shift, as early as possible.

7. After the festival

Every volunteer who completes three shifts receives a free weekend ticket for next year's festival. We will also send you a short survey, and your answers help us to improve the volunteer programme.

Thank you again, and enjoy the festival!
