Laboratory Safety Handbook

Marlow Research Institute · Second edition, valid from 1 September 2026

1 Introduction

1.1 Purpose

This handbook sets out the rules that keep everyone safe in the institute’s laboratories. It applies to staff, students and visitors from the moment they enter a laboratory.

1.2 Scope

The rules cover three areas of daily work.

  • Personal protective equipment
  • Handling, storage and disposal of chemicals
  • Emergency procedures and first aid

Never work alone in a laboratory after 18:00.

2.2 Care of equipment

Protective equipment must be checked before every use and replaced when it is damaged.

  1. Check the equipment before use
    1. look for cracks, holes and stains
    2. check that goggles seal against the face
  2. Store coats and goggles outside the laboratory
  3. Report damaged equipment to the safety officer

3 Chemical handling

3.1 Storage

Chemicals are stored by hazard class and never alphabetically. Flammable liquids belong in the ventilated cabinet next to the door.

3.2 Spills

Do not try to clean up a spill of an unknown substance.

In case of a small spill, proceed as follows:

  1. Warn the people around you and stop the work
  2. Put on gloves and goggles, then
    1. cover the spill with absorbent material
    2. sweep the material into a labelled bag
  3. Wash the area with water and soap

4.2 Evacuation

When the alarm sounds, leave the laboratory immediately and close the door behind you. Do not use the lifts, and do not return until the safety officer allows it.

Gather at the assembly point in the east car park and report to your group leader.
