Aurora 600 Air Purifier

Quiet, efficient air cleaning for living rooms and bedrooms

Cleaner air without the noise

The Aurora 600 removes dust, pollen and smoke particles from rooms of up to 45 square metres. Its three-stage filter captures 99.97% of particles larger than 0.3 microns.

A night mode lowers the fan to a whisper, so the unit can run while you sleep.

Care and maintenance

1.Switch off the unit and unplug it before any cleaning.
2.Clean the filters as follows:
(a)wash the pre-filter under lukewarm water once a month;
(b)let it dry completely before refitting it;
3.Replace the main filter when the indicator turns red.

Never wash the main filter, because water destroys the filter fibres.
