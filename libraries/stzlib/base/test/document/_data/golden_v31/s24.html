Rental Property Inspection Checklist

Use this form at the start and at the end of every tenancy. Both the tenant and the agent must sign it on the day of the inspection.

How to complete this form

  1. Walk through each room together with the tenant.
  2. Tick one box for each item: Good, Fair or Poor.
  3. Describe any damage in the notes column.
