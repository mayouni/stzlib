New play area opens on the Green

After two years of fundraising and three months of building work, the new play area on Millbrook Green opened on Saturday 10 October.

More than two hundred residents came to the opening, which was marked with a cake baked by the youngest members of the after-school club. The area has swings, a climbing frame for older children, a sand pit and a sheltered bench for parents and carers.

The project cost 48,000 pounds, of which more than a third was raised by residents themselves. The rest came from the housing association, a community grant and a local building firm that donated the safety surfacing.

“We wanted a place where children from every block could meet.”

The idea first came up at the association’s annual meeting in 2024, when parents complained that the nearest playground was across the main road. A working group of eight volunteers drew up a plan, asked children what they would like to see, and organised the sponsored walks, quiz nights and bake sales that paid for much of it.

The play area is open from 8 am until dusk. Please report any damage to the estate office, and help us keep it clean by using the bins at the gate.

Heating repairs: what happens next

The housing association has confirmed that the boilers in Ash and Beech Houses will be replaced before the end of January. Work will be carried out one floor at a time, and each flat will be without hot water for no more than one day.

Tenants will receive a letter at least a week before the work reaches their floor. Electric heaters will be lent to anyone who needs one, and priority will be given to households with young children or residents in poor health.
