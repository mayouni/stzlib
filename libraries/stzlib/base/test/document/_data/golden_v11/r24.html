Notes to the financial statements

1 Accounting policies

The financial statements have been prepared on the historical cost basis, except that land is shown at valuation. Income is recognised when the Trust is entitled to it and the amount can be measured reliably. Buildings are depreciated over fifty years and equipment over five years on a straight-line basis.
