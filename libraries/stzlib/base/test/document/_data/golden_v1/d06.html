Setting Up the Workshop Dust Extractor

This guide explains how to install the extractor in a small workshop. Read all the steps before you start.

Before you begin, make sure you have:

  • A flat floor area of at least one square metre
  • The following tools:
    • A 10 mm spanner
    • A Phillips screwdriver
    • A tape measure
  • A wall socket within three metres of the machine

Installation steps:

  1. Unpack the motor unit and the filter bag.
  2. Fit the filter bag to the outlet ring.
    1. Pull the bag over the ring until it is tight.
    2. Close the metal clamp around the bag.
  3. Connect the hose to the inlet on the front.
  4. Switch on the machine and listen for unusual noise.

Empty the filter bag when it is about two thirds full. A full bag reduces suction and can overheat the motor.
