Quarterly Rainfall Bulletin, Station K-12

This bulletin summarises the rainfall measured at Station K-12 during the third quarter of 2025. The station is located on a hill farm about four kilometres north of Tarnby.

Total rainfall for the quarter was 214 millimetres, well above the long-term average1. September was the wettest month of the quarter.
