Annual Maintenance Report 2025

1 Summary

This report describes the maintenance work carried out on the Fennick Bridge in 2025. All scheduled inspections were completed, and no structural defects were found.

1.1 Scope

The report covers the deck, the two piers and the drainage system. Lighting and road markings are covered by a separate report.

1.2 Method

Inspectors walked the full length of the deck in April and again in October. Each pier was examined from a boat at low tide.

2 Findings

The bridge remains in good condition overall.

2.1 Deck

Minor surface cracks were sealed on the eastern approach. The expansion joints showed normal wear for their age.

2.2 Drainage

Three drains near the western pier were blocked by leaves and were cleared in November. We recommend a second cleaning visit each autumn from 2026 onwards.

3 Budget

The total cost of the work was 48,200 euros, which is 6 percent below the approved budget.
