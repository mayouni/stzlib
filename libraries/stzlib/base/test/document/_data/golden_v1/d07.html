X200 Field Lantern User Manual

The X200 is a rechargeable lantern for camping, fieldwork and power cuts. This manual explains how to charge, use and store the lantern safely.

1 Charging

Charge the lantern fully before you use it for the first time. A full charge takes about four hours with the supplied cable.

2 Using the lantern

Press the power button once to switch on the lantern at low brightness. Each further press raises the brightness by one step, up to four steps.

Hold the power button for two seconds to switch the lantern off.

3 Storage and care

Store the lantern in a dry place at a temperature between 5 and 25 degrees Celsius. Recharge it at least once every three months, even when it is not in use.

Do not open the housing, because there are no parts inside that you can repair.
