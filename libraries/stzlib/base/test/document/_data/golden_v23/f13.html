Practical information

The Civic Centre is a ten-minute walk from Saltmarsh station. Badges must be worn at all times inside the venue.

  • Cloakroom on the ground floor, open from 08:00 to 18:00
  • Quiet room for prayer and rest on the first floor, next to Room 2
  • Step-free access through the east entrance on Harbour Road
  • Free drinking water at every landing
