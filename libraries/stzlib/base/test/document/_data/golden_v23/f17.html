Remarks

  1. Coliform bacteria were detected while E. coli was absent, which points to surface ingress rather than faecal contamination.

Interpretation

The sample does not meet the drinking water limits for coliform bacteria, iron and pH. We recommend disinfecting the borehole and taking a repeat sample after 14 days. Until a satisfactory result is obtained, water for drinking should be boiled or taken from a bottled supply.
