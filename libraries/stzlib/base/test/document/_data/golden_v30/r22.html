New Ferry Timetable Starts in May

By the Gazette team

From 4 May the harbour ferry will run every 30 minutes between the old quay and the island, seven days a week. The council confirmed the new timetable after a successful trial last autumn.

“The old boat was lovely, but the new one is on time.”

The first crossing leaves the old quay at 7:00 and the last returns at 22:30 during the summer season. Cyclists can now board without booking, and the larger vessel has room for twelve bicycles and four pushchairs.

Harbour clean-up day

Volunteers are needed for the spring clean-up of the slipway and the beach on Saturday 23 May. Refreshments will be served at the sailing club from noon.

Please bring the following with you:

  • Waterproof boots or wellingtons
  • Gloves, if you have them
  • A reusable bottle of water

Lighthouse open day

The old lighthouse will open its doors to visitors on Sunday 14 June for the first time in five years. Guided tours of the lamp room leave every twenty minutes, and the keepers’ cottage will house a small exhibition of photographs and charts.

Places on the tower are limited to fifteen people per tour. Tickets are free but must be collected from the harbour office in advance.

Your questions answered

How to book a crossing
  • 1) Choose your date and time on the council website.
  • 2) Select the number of passengers and bicycles.
  • 3) Pay by card or buy a weekly pass.
  • 4) Show the ticket on your phone when you board.
Notes from the harbour master

The harbour master reminds all boat owners that the speed limit inside the harbour is five knots. Moorings must be renewed before the end of April. Anyone who finds a lost lifebelt should hand it in at the harbour office.

Fares from May

Fares have not risen since 2023. Children under five travel free, and concession fares apply to holders of a council discount card.
