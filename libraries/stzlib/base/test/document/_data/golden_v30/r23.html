3. Conclusion

The sample meets the drinking-water standard for all parameters except iron, which exceeds the limit of 0.20 mg/l. The elevated iron content does not pose a health risk, but it can cause staining and a metallic taste.

4. Recommendations

  • i.
    Flush the kitchen tap for two minutes before drawing water for drinking.
  • ii.
    Inspect the internal pipework of the village hall for corrosion.
  • iii.
    Repeat the iron analysis on a new sample after the inspection.

5. Sampling and handling

The sample was collected by a trained sampler in a sterilised borosilicate bottle after the tap had been run for one minute. It was kept at 4 °C during transport. The results relate only to the sample as received.
