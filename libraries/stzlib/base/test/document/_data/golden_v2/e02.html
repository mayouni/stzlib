The Harbour Lane Gazette

New Bus Stop Opens on Quay Street

The long-awaited bus stop on Quay Street opened to passengers on the first Monday of April.

Route 14 now stops there every twenty minutes between six in the morning and ten at night.

Residents asked for a shelter with a bench, and the council has promised one before the summer.

Library Extends Its Opening Hours

The Harbour Lane library will stay open until eight o'clock on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

Volunteers will run a homework club for children aged eight to twelve during the extra hours.

Anyone who wants to help can leave their name at the front desk.

Spring Clean-Up Day

Our yearly clean-up day takes place on Saturday the 16th of May, starting at nine o'clock.

Gloves, bags and tea will be provided at the community hall.

Last year forty-two neighbours collected more than ninety bags of rubbish from the beach and the park.
