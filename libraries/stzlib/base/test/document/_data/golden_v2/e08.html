Annual Report of the Riverside Allotment Society

Membership

The society ended the year with 63 members, five more than last year. Eleven people are still on the waiting list for a plot.

FINANCES

Income from plot rents was 3,150 pounds, and expenses came to 2,870 pounds. The surplus will be kept as a reserve for repairing the water pipes.

Plot Inspections

Inspections took place in June and September. Four plot holders received a written reminder to clear weeds from the paths.

Next Year

The committee proposes to build a shared tool shed near the main gate. Members will vote on the proposal at the spring meeting.
