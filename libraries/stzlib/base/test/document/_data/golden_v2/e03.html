Restoring the Old Mill Pond

At a Glance

The pond covers about 1.8 hectares.

Work began in September 2025 and should end in June 2026.

The total budget is 214,000 euros.

Why the Pond Matters

The old mill pond is the last open water in the eastern part of the town. For decades it was a resting place for herons, ducks and the occasional kingfisher. Since the sluice gate collapsed, the pond has slowly filled with silt and reeds.

What the Work Involves

Contractors will first remove about four thousand cubic metres of silt. The sluice gate will then be rebuilt in oak, following the original drawings kept in the town archive. Finally, a footpath with two viewing platforms will be laid along the southern bank.

The restoration group meets on the first Thursday of every month in the parish hall, and its minutes are published on the notice board outside the library.
