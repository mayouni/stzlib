45. Declarations of interest

Councillor Brandt declared a non-pecuniary interest in item 49 as a member of the Eastmere Allotment Society.

46. Minutes of the previous meeting

RESOLVED that the minutes of the meeting held on 10 September 2026 be approved as a correct record and signed by the Mayor.

47. Mayor's announcements

  • The Mayor thanked the volunteers who supported the Heritage Open Days weekend, which attracted over 3,100 visitors.
  • The refurbished Wharf Street library will reopen on 2 November 2026.
  • The Remembrance Sunday parade will assemble at 10.15 am at the War Memorial.

48. Questions from members of the public

Two questions had been submitted in accordance with Standing Order 11. Mrs G. Pellow asked when the pedestrian crossing on Mill Lane would be repaired, and the Cabinet Member for Highways replied that the work was scheduled for the week beginning 19 October. Mr F. Adeyemi asked whether the Council would extend the opening hours of the recycling centre during the winter months.

The Cabinet Member for Environment undertook to provide a written answer within ten working days.
