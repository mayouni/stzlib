Summary

The Fund returned 2.9% in the third quarter, ahead of its policy benchmark by 0.4 percentage points. Gains came mainly from global equities and infrastructure, while government bonds were broadly flat.

The funding ratio improved from 94.3% to 96.1% over the quarter.

4. Outlook

The Investment Committee expects returns to moderate in the final quarter as interest rate cuts are already reflected in asset prices. No change to the strategic asset allocation is proposed before the triennial review in 2027.

Actions agreed by the Committee

  • Reduce the cash overweight towards the 3% target by the end of the year.
  • Complete the search for a second infrastructure manager.
  • Report on climate-related risks in the annual report for 2026.
