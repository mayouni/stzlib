Polio Vaccination Campaign

14 – 17 November 2026 · 14 – 17 ga Nuwamba 2026

Protect every child under five

All children under five years old will receive polio vaccine drops free of charge. The vaccine is safe and is given by mouth: just two drops. Vaccinate your child again even if he or she has been vaccinated before.

When and where

Health workers will visit every house from Saturday to Tuesday. You can also bring your child to one of the fixed posts listed below.

What parents should know

  • Keep your child's vaccination card and show it to the team.
  • A child who is sick can still be vaccinated.
  • Tell the team if a child in the house was not vaccinated.

After vaccination, the child's little finger will be marked with ink.

Kare kowane yaro ɗan ƙasa da shekara biyar

Za a ba duk yara 'yan ƙasa da shekara biyar digon maganin rigakafin cutar shan inna kyauta. Maganin ba shi da wata illa, kuma ana ba da shi ta baki: digo biyu kawai. Ku sake kawo ɗanku a yi masa rigakafi ko da an taɓa yi masa a baya.

Yaushe kuma ina

Ma'aikatan lafiya za su ziyarci kowane gida daga ranar Asabar zuwa ranar Talata. Kuna iya kawo ɗanku a ɗaya daga cikin wuraren da aka lissafa a ƙasa.

Abin da iyaye ya kamata su sani

  • Ku ajiye katin rigakafin ɗanku kuma ku nuna wa ma'aikata.
  • Yaro mara lafiya ma ana iya yi masa rigakafi.
  • Ku sanar da ma'aikata idan akwai yaron da ba a yi wa rigakafi ba a gidan.

Bayan an yi wa yaro rigakafi, za a sanya masa alama da tawada a ɗan yatsansa.
