Section B

Answer all the questions in this section. Diagrams are not drawn to scale.

  • 4. A straight line passes through the points A(−2, 5) and B(4, −7).
    • (a) Find the gradient of AB. [2]
    • (b) Find the equation of the line AB in the form y = mx + c. [2]
    • (c) Find the coordinates of the midpoint of AB. [2]
  • 5. A shop increases the price of a jacket by 20% and later reduces the new price by 15%.
    • (a) The original price was $80. Find the final price. [3]
    • (b) Find the overall percentage change in price. [2]
  • 6. In triangle PQR, PQ = 8 cm, QR = 11 cm and angle PQR = 52°.
    • (a) Calculate the length of PR. [4]
    • (b) Calculate the area of triangle PQR. [3]
