1. Background

The 2026 wet season input campaign was organised by the Union with its member cooperatives in four communes along the Dallol plains. Its aim was to place certified seed and fertiliser in the hands of producer groups before the first planting rains. Groups were selected on the basis of the 2025 harvest assessment and of their repayment record under the warrantage scheme.

Each group received millet and cowpea seed in proportion to its registered members, together with one bag of NPK for every two members and one bag of urea for every three. Seed varieties were chosen with the regional research station for their tolerance of short rainy seasons.

3. Observations

  • Distribution started three weeks earlier than in 2025 because seed was pre-positioned in the commune stores in May.
  • Women made up a larger share of members than in any previous campaign, mainly through the new market gardening groups.
  • Two distribution points in the Gaya Plateau commune could not be reached by truck after the heavy rains of 28 June; inputs were carried the last kilometres by cart.
  • No losses or thefts were reported, and all delivery notes were countersigned by the village chiefs.

4. Recommendations

  1. Keep the May pre-positioning of seed, and add a fourth commune store in the Gaya Plateau.
  2. Reduce urea quantities for groups farming the sandy soils of the plateau, where losses to leaching are high.
  3. Train group treasurers to record the planting date of each plot so that yields can be compared across varieties.

The Union will present the harvest results of this campaign at its general assembly in February 2027.
