<section_header_level_1><loc_48><loc_59><loc_171><loc_70>Why the Pond Matters</section_header_level_1>
<text><loc_48><loc_75><loc_273><loc_119>The old mill pond is the last open water in the eastern part of the town. For decades it was a resting place for herons, ducks and the occasional kingfisher. Since the sluice gate collapsed, the pond has slowly filled with silt and reeds.</text>
<section_header_level_1><loc_48><loc_128><loc_180><loc_139>What the Work Involves</section_header_level_1>
<text><loc_48><loc_144><loc_444><loc_171>Contractors will first remove about four thousand cubic metres of silt. The sluice gate will then be rebuilt in oak, following the original drawings kept in the town archive. Finally, a footpath with two viewing platforms will be laid along the southern bank.</text>
<text><loc_48><loc_177><loc_454><loc_194>The restoration group meets on the first Thursday of every month in the parish hall, and its minutes are published on the notice board outside the library.</text>
