<section_header_level_1><loc_38><loc_79><loc_83><loc_90>Overview</section_header_level_1>
<text><loc_38><loc_97><loc_254><loc_136>The AquaSense 300 measures volumetric water content and soil temperature at the root zone of field crops, orchards and school gardens. Its sealed probe has no exposed metal electrodes, so readings stay stable for several seasons in acidic or saline soils.</text>
<text><loc_38><loc_141><loc_250><loc_172>The sensor is installed by pushing it into a pilot hole made with the supplied auger. A single cable carries both power and data, which keeps installation simple for irrigation contractors and extension officers.</text>
