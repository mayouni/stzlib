<section_header_level_1><loc_25><loc_100><loc_71><loc_108>Background</section_header_level_1>
<text><loc_20><loc_111><loc_185><loc_136>Smallholder vegetable growers in the savanna zone irrigate by habit rather than by measurement. Over-irrigation wastes pumped water and fuel, while under-irrigation during fruit set cuts yields sharply.</text>
<text><loc_20><loc_139><loc_183><loc_158>Commercial tensiormen cost more than a season of fuel, so we tested whether a capaci sesor costing under five dollars could guide irrigation decisions.</text>
