The keyboard

Keys A S D F G H J K L play degrees 1–9 of the mode (degree 8 is the octave). Every tuning is the universe's declared one: Rast's half-flat third, slendro's stretched octave.

Live loops — edit while they play

Change a pattern and press Update: it lands on the next cycle, never in the middle of one. The grammar is MU3's: ~ [ ] [a, b] <a b> * / ? ! @.
